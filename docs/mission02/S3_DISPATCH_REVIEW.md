## Axis 2 — scope and simplicity

- **INFERENCE:** A mandatory permit-bound transport gateway is a deep module, not a global policy engine: deleting it restores the read/check-to-send race and permits direct adapters to bypass authorization. Typed preparation/effect claims are needed because raw arguments do not capture normalized equivalence. The design should consider one persisted claim/decision state machine rather than separate tables unless distinct lifecycles require both.
- **VERIFIED:** Additive revisions after `0002` are compatible with ADR-0007; `0001_baseline` is frozen and partial/nonconforming pre-existing schema must fail before DDL ([ADR-0007:21](/home/nill/.paseo/worktrees/0y1qsxa1/mission02-s3-dispatch-review/docs/adr/ADR-0007-frozen-baseline-and-c2-migration.md:21), [ADR-0007:29](/home/nill/.paseo/worktrees/0y1qsxa1/mission02-s3-dispatch-review/docs/adr/ADR-0007-frozen-baseline-and-c2-migration.md:29)). The proposal is additive if implemented in a new revision with compatibility checks. No migration was run.
- **VERIFIED / MISSING ORACLES:** The fake-provider and PostgreSQL race plan is strong, but must add: (1) pause worker A after `RUNNING`+reservation commit; worker B replays and commits `NEEDS_REVIEW`; resume A and assert zero transport calls; (2) Bridge retry/corrective and paginated second-egress after revoke, asserting the documented permit scope; (3) result reuse across two invocation IDs, asserting response refs, observation provenance, and measured budget are linked exactly once; (4) advisor prepare/dispatch byte and source-version equality; (5) one no-permit test at every raw adapter/transport entry, including acquisition, Retrohunt, advisor and Bridge.

## Verdict

**BLOCKED_DESIGN** — `IMPLEMENTABLE_NOW` is not justified until consume atomically verifies the persisted invocation/Bridge-attempt is still dispatchable, not merely that the envelope and reservation remain valid. Add the row lock/CAS and deterministic replay race oracle. Other listed gaps require explicit scope and provenance oracles before claiming all guarantees.
